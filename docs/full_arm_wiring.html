Potentiometer feedback

Each potentiometer uses all three terminals: one end to a regulated +5 V reference, the other end to common GND, and the center wiper directly to the assigned calibrated ADC input.

There is no resistor divider in the current build. The potentiometers use 5 V references, and the mechanical joint limits keep the calibrated wiper range below 3.3 V. This provides no electrical overvoltage protection if a mechanical stop, ground, or wiper connection fails. Never apply 5 V directly to an ESP32 ADC pin.

Commissioning order: keep motor power disconnected, verify common ground, measure each ADC node with a multimeter through the full mechanical range, confirm it never exceeds 3.3 V, then run the calibration firmware. Support the arm mechanically throughout calibration.
Boot warning: GPIO5 and GPIO15 are ESP32 strapping pins. If the board fails to boot with the drivers attached, disconnect those inputs during reset or revise the wiring and firmware together.
